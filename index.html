who we are.

we’re a couple of locals who’ve called hudson, colorado home for years. with a love for travel (Italy is our favorite!) but a serious dislike for the usual hotel scene, we decided to shake things up and create a better way to share our town with visitors.

fast forward 3 years, and we’re proud to be superhosts, welcoming guests and showing them the true charm of hudson. whether you’re here for a weekend or planning a longer stay, we’re here to make sure your experience feels like a home away from home.

we go beyond hotels.

dont worry, we fill it up before you get here.

we’ve made sure our spaces come with all the basics you'd expect from wifi to toilet paper, but we didn’t stop there.

each spot has its own fully equipped kitchen, so you can cook up your favorite meals, plus cozy workplaces if you need to get some work done. for some fun downtime, you’ll find a selection of board games like ping-pong or foosball to keep things lively.

oh, and did we mention the hot tub? perfect for unwinding after a day of exploring hudson. we’ve thought of everything to make your stay as comfortable and enjoyable as possible.

serving enough s p a c e for your party.

have a large crew? we’ve got you covered. our space can host up to 15 guests, making it perfect for keeping your group connected under one roof.

with plenty of room to relax, hang out, and create memories, you’ll find it’s the ideal spot for family reunions, friend trips, or any gathering that calls for a cozy home base.
